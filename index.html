01 / UNDERSTAND

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

Phishing

Fraudulent messages designed to trick victims into clicking malicious links, opening attachments or sharing sensitive information.

What Attackers Want

  • Passwords
  • Banking information
  • OTP or verification codes
  • Personal information
  • Access to accounts

How They Trick You

  • Fake urgency
  • Fear and threats
  • Impersonation
  • Fake rewards
  • False authority
02 / SOCIAL ENGINEERING

Know Their Tactics

Attackers often target human emotions instead of technical vulnerabilities.

01

Urgency

"Your account will be closed in 10 minutes!"

Tip: Stop and verify before taking action.
02

Fear

"Suspicious activity detected. Verify immediately."

Tip: Don't let fear make your decision.
03

False Authority

"This is your bank manager. Send the OTP now."

Tip: Verify the person's identity independently.
04

Reward

"Congratulations! You have won a ₹50,000 prize."

Tip: If it sounds too good to be true, verify it.
03 / DETECT

Spot the Red Flags

Before clicking a link or responding to a message, look for these warning signs.

Unknown Sender

The email comes from an unfamiliar or suspicious address.

Suspicious URL

The website address contains misspellings, strange domains or unexpected characters.

Unexpected Urgency

The message pressures you to act immediately.

Requests Sensitive Data

It asks for passwords, OTPs, card details or other confidential information.

Unexpected Attachment

An unexpected document or executable file is attached.

STOP • THINK • VERIFY

Never let pressure make you reveal sensitive information or click an unknown link.

06 / PROTECT YOURSELF

Build Strong Security Habits

Enable MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication adds another layer of protection beyond your password.

Use Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords or passphrases for important accounts and consider a password manager.

Verify Requests

Contact the organization through an official channel before sharing sensitive information.

Report Phishing

Report suspicious messages to your organization, email provider or appropriate authorities.
